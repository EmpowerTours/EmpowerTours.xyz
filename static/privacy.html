Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

EmpowerTours ("we", "our", "us") operates the EmpowerTours mobile application. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, phone number, display name, and wallet address when you create an account.
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates during active experience sessions to provide real-time tracking and route recording. Location is only collected while you are actively using an experience.
  • Photos: Photos you take at experience checkpoints are stored with location metadata.
  • Payment Information: Payment processing is handled by Apple (App Store), Google (Play Store), and Stripe. We do not store your credit card numbers.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, booking history, and session statistics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide real-time GPS tracking during tourism experiences
  • To process bookings and payments
  • To connect travelers with experience guides
  • To store your checkpoint photos and route history
  • To manage your subscription
  • To improve our services

Location Data

We collect precise GPS location only during active experience sessions that you explicitly start. Location tracking stops when you end the session. We do not track your location in the background when you are not using an experience. Your location history is visible only to you and your assigned guide.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Experience guides: Your real-time location during an active session
  • Payment processors: Stripe, Apple, and Google for transaction processing
  • Cloud infrastructure: AWS for data storage and hosting

Data Retention

Your account data is retained while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us. GPS location history and photos are retained until you delete them or close your account.

Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and secure storage practices. Authentication uses JWT tokens with expiration. Payment data is handled entirely by PCI-compliant processors.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data (route history, photos)
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection

Children's Privacy

EmpowerTours is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will notify you through the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
