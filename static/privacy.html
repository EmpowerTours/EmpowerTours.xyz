Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

EmpowerTours ("we", "our", "us") offers two separate products, and they handle data very differently. This policy covers both. Read the section that applies to you.

  • EmpowerTours Radio — our free music app on Google Play and the App Store. No account, no sign-in, and no personal data.
  • EmpowerTours account — travel and social features available on the web and in our full-featured app. These require an account.

Part 1 — EmpowerTours Radio (the music app)

This section describes the app published on Google Play and the App Store as EmpowerTours Radio. It is a free music player. It has no sign-in screen, no account, no purchases, and no advertising.

What we collect

One thing only: an anonymous play count. When a track starts, the app tells our server which track was played, identified solely by its track ID. This lets artists see how often their music is played.

This event contains no identifier of any kind — no account, no device ID, no advertising ID, no username. It cannot be linked back to you or to your device, and we cannot tell which plays came from the same person.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information
  • No location or GPS data of any kind
  • No photos, files, contacts, calendar, or microphone access
  • No device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • No payment information — there is nothing to buy in the app

Advertising and third-party tracking

The Radio app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software. We do not sell or share any data with third parties, and no data is used to track you across apps or websites.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers, including music streaming, is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Deleting your data

Because the Radio app collects nothing that identifies you, there is no personal data associated with you to delete. If you have questions, contact us at admin@empowertours.xyz. See our data deletion page for details.

Music and reporting content

Tracks are published by independent artists, who confirm they hold the rights to the music they upload. Lyrics are not moderated and may contain explicit language, which is why the app is age-rated accordingly. To report a track or raise a rights/DMCA concern, email support@empowertours.xyz.

Part 2 — EmpowerTours account (travel and social features)

This section applies only if you create an EmpowerTours account to use our travel and social features. It does not apply to the EmpowerTours Radio app described above, which has no accounts.

Information we collect

  • Account information: Email address, phone number, display name, and wallet address when you create an account.
  • Facebook Login data: If you choose "Continue with Facebook", we receive your Facebook user ID, name, email address, and public profile picture from Meta. We do not request access to your friends list, posts, or any other Facebook content.
  • Location data: GPS coordinates during active experience sessions, to provide real-time tracking and route recording. Location is collected only while you are actively using an experience.
  • Photos: Photos you take at experience checkpoints are stored with location metadata.
  • Payment information: Payment processing is handled by Apple, Google, and Stripe. We do not store your card numbers.
  • Usage data: App interactions, booking history, and session statistics.

How we use it

  • To provide real-time GPS tracking during tourism experiences
  • To process bookings and payments
  • To connect travelers with experience guides
  • To store your checkpoint photos and route history
  • To manage your subscription
  • To improve our services

Location data

We collect precise GPS location only during active experience sessions that you explicitly start. Tracking stops when you end the session. We do not track your location in the background. Your location history is visible only to you and your assigned guide.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Experience guides: your real-time location during an active session
  • Payment processors: Stripe, Apple, and Google, for transaction processing
  • Cloud infrastructure: AWS, for data storage and hosting

Retention and deletion

Account data is retained while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data — including data received from Facebook — at any time. Instructions are on our data deletion page. If you remove EmpowerTours from your Facebook account, Facebook notifies our deletion endpoint and we delete your account automatically.

Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and secure storage practices. Authentication uses JWT tokens with expiration. Payment data is handled entirely by PCI-compliant processors.

Your rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data (route history, photos)
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection

Applies to both

Children's privacy

EmpowerTours is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The EmpowerTours Radio app streams music from independent artists whose lyrics are not moderated, and is age-rated for mature audiences.

Changes

We may update this policy and will notify you through the app or on this page. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

Content or rights concerns: support@empowertours.xyz
