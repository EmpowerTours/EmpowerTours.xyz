User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: April 8, 2026

EmpowerTours takes user privacy seriously. You may request deletion of your personal data — including any data we received from Meta/Facebook when you signed in with Facebook — at any time, using one of the methods below.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the EmpowerTours mobile app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.
  4. Your account, profile, sessions, photos, and Facebook linkage are permanently removed within 30 days.

Option 2 — Email request

Send an email from the address associated with your account to privacy@empowertours.xyz with the subject line Delete my account. Include either your registered email or your Facebook display name so we can locate your record. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

Option 3 — Facebook Data Deletion Callback

If you remove the EmpowerTours app from your Facebook account, Facebook will automatically notify our deletion endpoint and we will delete all data linked to your Facebook user ID.

Deletion callback URL (used by Facebook only): https://api.empowertours.xyz/auth/facebook/data-deletion

To remove the app from Facebook:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find EmpowerTours in the list and click Remove.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (email, display name, profile photo, wallet address)
  • Your Facebook user ID and any profile fields received from Facebook
  • Your bookings, experience sessions, GPS route history, and checkpoint photos
  • Your subscription metadata (payment processor records are governed by Stripe / Apple / Google)

Tracking your request

When you submit a deletion request via the app or email, we issue you a confirmation code. You can check the status by emailing privacy@empowertours.xyz with your code.

Contact

For any privacy or deletion questions:
Email: privacy@empowertours.xyz
