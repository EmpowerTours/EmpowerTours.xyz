User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: July 22, 2026

How you delete your data depends on which EmpowerTours product you use. Find yours below.

EmpowerTours Radio (the music app)

This is the free music app published on Google Play and the App Store. It has no account and no sign-in screen.

The Radio app does not collect any personal data, so there is no personal data associated with you to delete.

The only thing the app sends us is an anonymous play count: when a track starts, we record which track was played, identified solely by its track ID. That event carries no account, no device identifier, and no advertising identifier. Because nothing links it to you or your device, we have no way to identify "your" data — and equally, no way for anyone else to.

To remove the app entirely, uninstall it from your device. Nothing is left behind on our servers that refers to you.

Questions about the Radio app and privacy: admin@empowertours.xyz.
To report a track or raise a rights/DMCA concern: support@empowertours.xyz.

EmpowerTours account (travel and social features)

This applies only if you created an EmpowerTours account for travel and social features, on the web or in our full-featured app. It does not apply to the Radio app above.

You may request deletion of your personal data — including any data we received from Meta/Facebook when you signed in with Facebook — at any time, using one of the methods below.

Option 1 — Email request

Send an email from the address associated with your account to admin@empowertours.xyz with the subject line Delete my account. Include either your registered email or your Facebook display name so we can locate your record. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

Option 2 — Delete from inside the app

If your version of the EmpowerTours app includes account features, sign in and go to Profile → Settings → Account, then tap Delete my account and confirm. Your account, profile, sessions, photos, and Facebook linkage are permanently removed within 30 days.

Option 3 — Facebook Data Deletion Callback

If you remove the EmpowerTours app from your Facebook account, Facebook automatically notifies our deletion endpoint and we delete all data linked to your Facebook user ID.

Deletion callback URL (used by Facebook only): https://api.empowertours.xyz/auth/facebook/data-deletion

To remove the app from Facebook:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find EmpowerTours in the list and click Remove.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (email, display name, profile photo, wallet address)
  • Your Facebook user ID and any profile fields received from Facebook
  • Your bookings, experience sessions, GPS route history, and checkpoint photos
  • Your subscription metadata (payment processor records are governed by Stripe / Apple / Google)

Tracking your request

When you submit a deletion request, we issue a confirmation code. You can check the status by emailing admin@empowertours.xyz with your code.

Contact

For any privacy or deletion question:
Email: admin@empowertours.xyz
