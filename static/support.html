Support

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Need help with EmpowerTours? Email admin@empowertours.xyz and we will reply within two business days. The answers below cover the questions we get most often.

Fastest help: include your device model, your operating system version, the app version (shown on the About screen) and the name of the track, if the problem involves one.

EmpowerTours Radio (the music app)

Do I need an account?

No. EmpowerTours Radio is free, has no sign-in screen, and has nothing to buy. Open the app and press play.

The music will not play

  • Check that your device is online — the catalog and the audio both stream over the internet.
  • Check that your device is not muted and that the volume is up.
  • Force-quit the app and reopen it. The catalog is refreshed on launch.
  • On a restricted network (some office, school, or hotel Wi-Fi), streaming may be blocked. Try mobile data.
  • Still stuck? Confirm our servers are up by opening the web player in your browser. If that plays and the app does not, email us.

Playback stops when I leave the app

EmpowerTours Radio supports background playback and lock-screen controls. If audio stops when you switch apps, check that your device's battery-saver or low-power mode is not suspending background audio.

The catalog looks empty or a track is missing

The catalog is built from tracks that artists publish themselves, so it changes as artists release and update work. If the list is empty for more than a few minutes, that is a fault on our side — email us and we will look at it.

How do I report a track?

Email admin@empowertours.xyz with the track name and artist. We handle rights and DMCA claims, offensive-content reports, and takedown requests at the same address. Include enough detail to identify the track and, for a rights claim, a statement of what you own.

Lyrics contain explicit language

Tracks come from independent artists and lyrics are not moderated, which is why the app is age-rated for mature audiences. Report anything that breaks the law or our terms at the address above.

Artists

How do I get my music on EmpowerTours?

Artists publish their own tracks and keep ownership of their work. To be set up as an artist, email admin@empowertours.xyz and tell us who you are and where your music currently lives.

Where do I see how often my tracks are played?

Play counts are anonymous totals per track. Ask us for your numbers at the address above.

EmpowerTours account (travel and social features)

Travel and social features are optional, require a free account, and are being rolled out separately from the music app.

I cannot sign in

  • If you signed up with an email address, make sure you are using the same address and check your spam folder for any verification mail.
  • If you signed in with Facebook, make sure you are signed in to the same Facebook account and that you have not removed EmpowerTours from your Facebook app settings.
  • If you signed in with a wallet, make sure the wallet is unlocked and on the correct network.

If none of that helps, email admin@empowertours.xyz from the address on the account.

How do I delete my account and my data?

See the data deletion page. You can request deletion at any time and we will confirm when it is done.
