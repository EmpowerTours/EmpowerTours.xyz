    05 Hire us on your repo · coming next

    Pay per merged task, not per hour.

    Connect your GitHub, fund a project balance in MON, and we ship pull requests into your repo. You merge what you accept; only accepted work is paid.

    step 1 · github

    Connect your repo

    Install the EmpowerTours GitHub App on the repos you pick. We never ask for your password, and you can revoke access in one click.

    step 2 · balance

    Fund the project

    Deposit MON into the project's escrow contract. The balance is on-chain and visible; unused funds withdraw back to you at any time.

    step 3 · ship

    Merge and release

    We quote each task, open a PR, you review and merge. The merge releases that task's payment. Nothing else moves your money.

    Each task gets a fixed price before work starts. You pay only when you merge its pull request, so you never pay for time you can't check or work you didn't accept.

    Scope your project with Claude ↗Or send a short brief

    The scoper runs on your own Claude plan: Claude splits your idea into priced, PR-sized tasks and sends us the brief. Free for you, no API key.
